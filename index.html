Privacy Policy

App: Crossing the Roads (com.aggames.crossingtheroads)
Developer: AG Games
Effective date: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Crossing the Roads (“the app”), an Android game by AG Games (“we”, “us”).

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location or any other personal details ourselves. We do not run our own servers for this app.

The app stores your best score on your device only. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use information from your device, such as:

  • your Android advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and software details (model, OS version, language, screen size),
  • information about how ads are shown and interacted with (impressions, clicks, ad views).

This information is collected and processed by Google and its partners under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your personal data from them.

The app shows three kinds of ads: a banner at the bottom of the screen, a full-screen ad after some games, and an optional rewarded video you can watch to continue a game.

Learn more:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

3. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): if you are in one of these regions, the app shows a consent form when you first open it. You can change your choice later using the “Privacy settings” button on the start screen, which appears where it is required.
  • Advertising ID: on your Android device, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device) to delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings to remove your saved best score.

4. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, please contact us at the email below.

5. Data sharing and security

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone, because we do not collect it. Data handled by Google AdMob is covered by Google’s policies linked above.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with a new effective date at the top.
